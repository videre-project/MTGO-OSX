# Brand Assets

`MTGO.ico` is the unchanged application icon shipped with the official MTGO ClickOnce client (SHA-256 `32976494b413f5e7b95fcb547b03dd24846ce25701fb23d9da24a43a05e2aec4`). It provides MTGO branding inside the launcher window. `//tools:icons` places the same mark on a charcoal background to generate `GameIcon.icns`, used throughout setup, launch and gameplay by the native app and Wine's window processes.

`Fonts/Inter.ttf` is [Inter from Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter) (Git blob `047c92f6e2212473dc436020afed689527076d44`), the family used on [videreproject.com](https://videreproject.com/). Its Open Font License is included in `Fonts/OFL.txt` and in the app bundle.

`MTGO-Background.jpg` is the unchanged dark page background from the [official MTGO website](https://www.mtgo.com/images/home/home-bg.jpg?v=1699732767). In MTGO-OSX, this artwork is cropped and shaded in SwiftUI to keep progress and controls legible. Magic: The Gathering artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
